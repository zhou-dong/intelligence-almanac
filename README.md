# intelligence-almanac

This repo holds the facts of the history of intelligence — the real biology, neuroscience, and AI/ML history (chemotaxis, perceptrons, backpropagation, TD learning, CNNs, reinforcement learning, and the human research history behind each) underlying the alchemist-ai narrative series. This repo is the source of truth for chapter structure and organizes chapters by shared period/organism, not the other way around — alchemist-ai's chapters should follow this repo's structure, not vice versa.

Project-agnostic facts about deep time and the domains of life (geologic eras, LUCA, bacteria/archaea/eukaryotes) live in the sibling repo `grimoire`, not here.

## Confidence tags

Every checkable claim is tagged with one of:

- **established** — settled scientific/historical consensus
- **plausible** — a reasonable inference, a leading hypothesis among competing ones, or a well-supported simplification — not yet settled or precisely sourced
- **speculative** — a narrative framing or simplification, not a claim about documented science or history

## Files

One file per chapter, each covering the facts specific to that chapter. A chapter covers one shared period and organism; when covering that much material in one file would get too long, it's split into sub-chapters (e.g. `ch2.1-...md`, `ch2.2-...md`) that still share the same period/organism. Facts shared across chapters, or shared with a future project, belong in `grimoire` instead.
